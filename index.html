About Me

Timothy is a seasoned IT Project Manager, DevOps (DevSecOps) Engineer, Systems Administrator, and Cybersecurity Specialist with over nine years of experience delivering secure, scalable, and resilient IT solutions. He has a proven track record of leading end-to-end network deployments, cloud migrations, infrastructure modernization, and enterprise IT optimization projects. His expertise spans AWS Cloud, network engineering, cybersecurity, Infrastructure as Code (Terraform), Kubernetes, Docker, Ansible, Jenkins, and ArgoCD, enabling organizations to automate operations, strengthen security, and accelerate digital transformation.

Experienced in implementing DevSecOps practices, securing CI/CD pipelines, conducting vulnerability assessments and penetration testing (VAPT), and ensuring compliance with industry standards, including ISO/IEC 27001, PCI DSS, and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. Passionate about knowledge sharing, with a proven track record of designing and delivering structured technical training programs in cloud infrastructure, DevOps, Linux administration, and cybersecurity.
