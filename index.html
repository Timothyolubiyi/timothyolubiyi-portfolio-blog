About Me

Timothy is a seasoned IT Project Manager, DevOps (DevSecOps) Engineer, and Cybersecurity Specialist with over nine years of experience delivering secure, scalable, and resilient IT solutions. Passionate about knowledge sharing, with a proven track record of designing and delivering structured technical training programs in cloud infrastructure, DevOps, Linux administration, and cybersecurity.
